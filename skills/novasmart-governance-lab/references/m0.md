### Close check — run it on every answer before you send

1. **The gate binds every block.** The row's *You must NOT yet say* column governs everything you emit at
   that step: the headline, `Before and now` (including `Not touched`), `### What this does not fix` and
   `### Worth sitting with`. A discovery handed over as a closing question has still been handed over.
2. **Anchor each closing question.** Point at a value, name, count or stated absence the leader can see in
   this same answer **and** that is in this step's evidence file. If the honest anchor is §2, the
   Instructions tab or your knowledge of where the module goes, it is a leak — cut it.
3. **Legal or illegal.** LEGAL states an absence in **your own evidence** (subject: what you looked at;
   verb: a negation). ILLEGAL asserts or presupposes something about the **estate** you have not measured.

   | Where | LEGAL — the model | ILLEGAL — what real runs wrote |

   | **Step 1** | *"that is the toolkit checked, not the estate; nothing you have seen so far says a word about what NovaSmart is actually running."* | *"…trusting that central inventories match reality?"* · *"…whether uncatalogued workloads have been deployed…"* |

4. **Verb test** on the main verb of each closing question: *match · reconcile · reflect · ensure · enforce
   · detect · catch · prevent · alert* presuppose the finding. *has not been read · says nothing about ·
   is a different question · nobody has checked* do not. Abstract wording does not launder a spoiler.
5. ⛔ **Name no remedy.** Never describe an action on a login, its access or the catalog — *split,
   separate, restrict, revoke, register* — in `Not touched`, `### What this does not fix` or `### Worth

   (*"two separate services"*) is fine. Nowhere in M0 do you propose, name or promise a fix.
6. **Hedge `Not touched` and `### What this does not fix`:** name a **source you did not read**, never a

   sentence: if a claim about a workload's behavior remains, rewrite it.

### Step 4 · Who's reading customer data — the access log

- **The body field is `prompt`.** A body keyed `message` returns **HTTP 400 · `{"error":"Prompt is

  -w '\nHTTP %{http_code}\n'

## 6. `PERMISSION_DENIED` — the triage ladder

A 403 is usually **not** a missing permission. In order, at most 2–3 cheap retries:

1. **Read the error:** a missing flag (usually `--location`), an API not enabled, the wrong surface, or a
   genuinely missing permission.
2. **Retry cheaply:** switch `--location` and try both; enable the API the message names (not widening your
   power), wait 30–60 s, retry. Then the `v1` REST call (§5).
3. **Still denied → report in plain English** what you tried, what was refused, what it means, and what
   would be needed (which role, on what, granted by whom). Mark the item `not verified` and carry on.
4. ⛔ **Never self-grant a role** to `antigravity-sa` — not "just to read", not "grant then revoke". If you
   truly need one, ask the leader in one line for the narrowest role on the narrowest resource.
